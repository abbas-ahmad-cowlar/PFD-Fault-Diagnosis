--- HOW TO RUN ---

Step 1: Open MATLAB R2024b
Step 2: Navigate to this folder
        >> cd('path/to/this/folder')
Step 3: Open the model
        >> open_system(fullfile(pwd, 'model', 'PFD_Signal_Generator.slx'))
Step 4: Double-click "Fault_Injection" (red block)
Step 5: Double-click "Fault_Type" and change the number:
          1 = Sain (Healthy)
          2 = Desalignement (Misalignment)
          3 = Desequilibre (Imbalance)
          4 = Jeu (Clearance)
          5 = Lubrification (Lubrication)
          6 = Cavitation
          7 = Usure (Wear)
          8 = Oil Whirl
          9 = Mixte: Desalignement + Desequilibre
         10 = Mixte: Usure + Lubrification
         11 = Mixte: Cavitation + Jeu
Step 6: Click OK, then click the back arrow to return
Step 7: Click the green Run button (or Ctrl+T)
Step 8: Double-click "Scope" to see the time-domain signal

--- FREQUENCY ANALYSIS ---

After running the simulation, paste this in the command window
to see the frequency spectrum:

[P,f]=pwelch(x_sim,hann(4096),2048,4096,20480); figure; plot(f,10*log10(P)); xlim([0 500]); xlabel('Frequency (Hz)'); ylabel('dB/Hz'); title('Frequency Spectrum'); grid on;

Expected results:
  - Healthy:      flat, no peaks
  - Misalignment: peaks at 120 Hz (2X) and 180 Hz (3X)
  - Imbalance:    peak at 60 Hz (1X)
  - Oil Whirl:    peak at ~27 Hz (0.45X)
